2. Service Area & Eligibility

CostlyCart is a local, on-demand 3D printing service currently operating exclusively for Colorado School of Mines students and residents of the Golden, CO area. Orders from outside this area will not be accepted at this time.

There is no minimum order quantity. We accept orders for single parts as well as small batch runs, subject to print queue availability.

3. Order Process & Quotes

Submitting an order form does not constitute a confirmed order. Once your form is received, CostlyCart will review your submitted file details and send a written quote to your provided email address. No printing begins until you explicitly confirm the quote in writing.

Quotes are calculated using the following pricing structure:

  • A flat $2.50 base service fee per order
  • $0.015 per gram of filament used, including all supports and rafts, as reported by the slicer
  • $0.75 per hour of print time, rounded up to the nearest full hour, as reported by the slicer

The final quoted price may differ from any estimate if the submitted file requires significant supports, dense infill, or other factors that affect print time or material use. All such differences will be communicated before printing begins.

4. Turnaround Time

CostlyCart targets a 24-hour turnaround from the moment a quote is confirmed by the customer. This is a goal, not a guarantee.

Turnaround times may be extended under the following circumstances:

  • High order volume or a full print queue at the time of confirmation
  • Prints with an estimated duration exceeding 20 hours
  • Print failures requiring a reprint
  • Equipment maintenance or unexpected hardware issues
  • Orders placed on weekends — these will be quoted and scheduled on the following Monday

If your order cannot be completed within 24 hours, CostlyCart will notify you by email as soon as possible with a revised estimated completion time. We appreciate your understanding — this is a small, one-person operation and we will always communicate delays promptly rather than leave you without an update.

Parts are provided as-is.

PLA, the material currently offered, is a general-purpose plastic suitable for prototypes, models, and light functional use. It is not suitable for high-heat environments (above approximately 60°C), prolonged UV exposure, or safety-critical structural applications.

CostlyCart is not liable for the structural integrity, mechanical performance, or fitness for purpose of any printed part. Payment is due upon pickup. Because 3D printing is a custom manufacturing service, orders cannot be cancelled or refunded once printing has begun. If you need to cancel, contact us immediately — if printing has not yet started, we will accommodate the cancellation at no charge.

If a part fails to print correctly due to a machine or operator error on our end, CostlyCart will reprint the part at no additional cost.
